Controller consoles

Choose the firmware on your controller. Join its Wi-Fi access point and the console connects automatically. Away from Wi-Fi, choose Connect BLE inside the console. For DRV8871 or TMC2209 controls, choose a dispenser console; motor support must also be enabled in firmware.
