Coordinate-triggered routines

Coordinate sequence is stopped. Feed MAVLink UDP or GeoPosition updates before starting.

Start authorizes automatic arming at each next point. Its routine runs once when inside the radius, then the sequence advances. Position loss stops the sequence and output. Restart explicitly starts again at point 1.

MAVLink input: UDP port 14550, system/component 1/1, GPS_RAW_INT and GLOBAL_POSITION_INT. A custom bridge can POST latitude,longitude,accuracyMeters,ageMs as plain text to /api/position. Updates must stay fresh throughout the routine.
